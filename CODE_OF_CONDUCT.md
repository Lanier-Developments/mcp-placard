# Code of conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/),
version 2.1.

In short: be respectful, assume good faith, argue about schemas and taxonomy rather than
about people, and accept that a maintainer's decision on a rule may go against you and
still be recorded with your evidence attached.

Reports of unacceptable behaviour go to the maintainers through GitHub's private
vulnerability reporting for this repository, which reaches the same people confidentially:
https://github.com/Lanier-Developments/mcp-placard/security/advisories/new
